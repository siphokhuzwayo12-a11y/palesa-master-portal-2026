01 · The strategic decision

One marriage.
Sequenced execution.

The SCRP is not presented as two unrelated ventures. It is a single circular-resource platform with implementation layers. Bio-Solar receives Phase-One implementation priority because it can establish the first operating infrastructure node; Green Minerals remains a principal strategic pillar and advances in parallel toward its own validation and engagement gates.

09 · SCAPUSH evidence discipline

Precision before promotion.

The marriage document distinguishes between what is confirmed, sourced, calculated, assumed and still requires validation. Patent, technical, financial and regulatory claims are not promoted beyond their evidence status.

CONFIRMED

Directly evidenced and accepted within the working record.

SOURCED

Supported by a named external or documentary source.

CALCULATED

Derived transparently from defined inputs and assumptions.

ASSUMED

Working premise requiring subsequent confirmation.

VALIDATION REQUIRED

Technical, commercial, legal or regulatory verification outstanding.

REGULATORY DEPENDENT

Outcome depends on site-specific authority and applicable requirements.
