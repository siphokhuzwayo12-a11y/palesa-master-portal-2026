4. Technical Methodology & Operations

Zero-Mortality Microclimate & Biomass
  • Hydronic HVAC Control: Maintains 28°C–32°C zones, achieving 0% cold-snap mortality.
  • Automated Environmental Controls: Extracts ammonia and moisture to mitigate respiratory hazards.
  • Biomass Co-Digestion: Manure & residues at C:N 25:1–30:1 yield 60–70% CH4 concentration.
  • Phase Separation: Produces liquid fertilizer and solid soil conditioners.
ArIoT Telemetry & SCADA Architecture
  • LoRaWAN Sensor Sink: Monitors temperature, gas/pH, pressure, and battery voltages.
  • Industrial SCADA: Triggers solenoid diverters to isolate faults without interrupting operations.
