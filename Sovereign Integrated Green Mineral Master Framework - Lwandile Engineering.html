1. Strategic Framework & Core Vision

Framework Objective

Establish decentralized, islanded microgrid hubs that integrate continuous bio-solar baseload energy with localized green mineral refining, decoupling critical processing from vulnerable utility grids.

Operational Scope

Deploying closed-loop anaerobic digestion co-generation, solar PV arrays, BESS buffer storage, and decarbonized thermal refining across the Kinross/Secunda industrial corridors.

2. Operational Pillars of Sovereignty

1. Energy Sovereignty

  • Continuous Baseload: Anaerobic biogas CHP paired with Solar PV guarantees 24/7 power.
  • BESS Balancing: High-density lithium buffer storage manages peak loads and system frequency.

2. Green Mineral Beneficiation

  • In-Situ Refining: Localized processing of critical minerals (Lithium, PGM, Manganese).
  • Decarbonized Thermal Loops: Direct heat recovery integration reduces energy costs.
