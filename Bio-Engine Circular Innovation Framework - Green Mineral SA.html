BIO-ENGINE CIRCULAR INNOVATION FRAMEWORK

National Strategic Infrastructure Model | Public-Private Partnership Execution

1. Executive Summary

Core Objective: Strategic deployment of an advanced 3-phase bio-engine recovery process designed to transform construction, demolition, and glass waste streams across South African municipalities into high-value engineered aggregates.

Strategic Deployment: Initial operational rollout targets high-density nodes (Johannesburg and Durban) utilizing existing municipal tariff models, scaling to 400 specialized processing facilities across 22 primary municipal zones over a 15-year horizon.

Impact Target: Redirection of ~10,000,000 annual tons of inert waste from overburdened landfills, absolute mitigation of illegal dumping proliferation, and direct creation of 7,200 specialized technical and operational jobs.

2. Technical & Strategic Glossary

Bio-Engine Innovation (Zero-Waste Processing)
A proprietary 3-phase industrial recovery model operating under patented zero-waste processing methodology to reclaim, treat, and synthesize C&D (Construction and Demolition) waste and glass.
Inert Granulate Synthetics
High-grade recycled aggregate materials produced via multi-stage crushing and bio-processing, offered at a competitive 10–15% discount compared to virgin quarry-extracted materials for public and private infrastructure projects.
PPP Structure (NewCo)
A co-funded Public-Private Partnership entity established between Green Mineral, financial sponsor Alseco, and the Industrial Development Corporation (IDC, capped at 49% equity participation), alongside local entities like Pikitup.
Parasocial Draft Convention
The binding governance and regulatory protocol submitted to local and national authorities (e.g., Ministry of Environment, prot. MCE211993) to formalize flow-control by-laws, landfill diversion mandates, and compulsory off-take of recycled aggregates in public tender works.
Landfill Airspace Remediation
The systematic diversion of high-volume inert aggregates from municipal dumpsites, extending landfill operational lifespans and removing the public tax burden associated with illegal site cleanups.
