Slide 1: Problem Statement & Proposed Solution

The Municipal Crisis

  • Rubble & Glass Accumulation: Over 10,000,000 tons/year of C&D rubble and waste glass clog municipal airspace.
  • Landfill Congestion: Rapidly depleting landfill capacity across major metros like eThekwini and Pikitup jurisdictions.
  • Environmental & Health Risks: Uncontrolled illegal dumping, toxic leachate runoff, and respirable silica dust threats.

Strategic PPP Intervention

  • Gate Intercept: Intercepting waste streams directly at landfill entry points and industrial corridors.
  • Vitrification & Recovery: 100% recycling into high-grade aggregates, architectural tiles, and glass-ceramics.
  • Zero-Grid Dependency: Bio-solar thermal micro-grids powering localized smart EV DC charging nodes.

Slide 4: Methodology & Operational Architecture

  1. Reception Yard & Weighbridge: Automated vehicle weighing, payload validation, and municipal tipping tariff capture.
  2. Primary Crushing: Heavy mechanical crushing of incoming concrete rubble, masonry, and demolition debris.
  3. Secondary Sorting: Magnetic extraction of scrap metals and optical separation of glass and mineral fines.
  4. Dust Suppression Domes & Vitrification: Air-scrubbed positive-pressure domes and high-temperature bio-solar kilns (>1,200°C) for zero silica dust and glass ceramic sintering.
  5. High-Value Outputs: Certified recycled aggregate, glass-ceramics, acid-resistant bricks, and thermal energy for agricultural bio-domes.
  6. Skills Transfer & TVET Training: Integrated TVET-accredited niche training facilities offering structured skills transfer in advanced vitrification and plant operations.
  7. Energy Spine: Bio-Solar PV + BESS + Local EV Mobility Charging: Self-sustained micro-grid providing 0% grid dependency and powering accessible, smart DC fast-charging points tailored for local municipal, commercial, and public mobility.
